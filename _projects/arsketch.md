## Paper

**ARSketch: Sketch-Based User Interface for Augmented Reality Glasses**  
ACM Multimedia 2020

## Problem

Design an intuitive user interface for AR glasses that goes beyond conventional menu-driven interaction and supports more natural input in immersive settings.

## Method

ARSketch explores sketch-based interaction for AR glasses, enabling users to draw sketches in air as an interaction primitive for communicating intent and controlling the system.

## My Role

I co-authored the paper and contributed to the design and development of the sketch-based interaction concept for augmented reality glasses.
